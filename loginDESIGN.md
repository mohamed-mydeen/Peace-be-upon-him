## Brand & Style

The design system crafts a sanctuary of stillness, reverence, and intellectual clarity. Designed for mindful Muslims, scholars, and spiritual seekers, the interface evokes the quiet grandeur of midnight prayers (*Tahajjud*) beneath architectural domes illuminated by warm lanterns.

The design movement merges **Atmospheric Night Photography** with **Refined Frosted Glassmorphism** and **Contemporary Editorial Structure**:
- **Deep Obsidian Immersion:** Interfaces emerge out of rich, pitch-black and deep-slate shadows rather than sterile grays, amplifying glowing architectural elements.
- **Luminescent Accentuation:** Sacred points of focus are anointed with brushed antique gold and veiled emerald green glow, mimicking soft minaret illumination against night skies.
- **Editorial Reverence:** Massive, condensed architectural headlines sit alongside crystalline, highly legible body typography, delivering an experience that feels simultaneously historic and effortlessly modern.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from nocturnal sacred architecture:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` — Antique Dome Gold):** Reserved for key focus markers, active states, active tab indicators, and sacred milestones. Used with deliberate restraint to maintain visual weight and reverence.
- **Secondary (`#1B4D3E` — Deep Emerald):** Evokes heritage tiling and lush evening courtyards. Deployed for subtle background gradients, secondary badges, contextual tags, and focused interactive surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#8FA382` — Sage Mist):** A desaturated herbal tone used for secondary metadata, subtle borders, verse categorization, and calming status cues.
- **Neutral Base (`#0D0F12` — Obsidian Night):** The primary canvas ground, supplemented by tonal surface variations:
  - Base Ground: `#080A0C`
  - Elevated Container / Sheet: `#13171C`
  - Frosted Modal Surface: `rgba(19, 23, 28, 0.78)`
- **Content / Foreground Hierarchy:**
  - High-Emphasis Content: `#FBFBF8` (Crisp Warm Ivory)
  - Medium-Emphasis / Subtitles: `#A4ACB9` (Cool Slate)
  - Ghost / Dividers: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes monumental presence with human-scale readability:

- **Display & Headings (`Barlow Condensed`):** Channeling vertical architectural minarets and stone inscriptions. Rendered with uppercase styling or tight, deliberate kerning for chapter titles, prayer callouts, and screen headers.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, clear geometry engineered for extended night-mode reading without eye fatigue. Generous line heights ensure uninterrupted flow through Hadith narrations, translations, and historical context.
- **Verse & Arabic Integration Guidance:** Arabic calligraphic text should sit alongside body tokens with a +4px relative scale adjustment to match visual optical density.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid prioritizes thumb reach, gesture ergonomics, and peaceful negative space.

- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout framed by `1.25rem` margin. Interactive actions anchor to the bottom 35% of the viewport. Full-bleed background imagery bleeds into safe areas, overlaid with gradient vignettes.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure with centered sheet containers (max width 600px for readings).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column restrained grid with sticky navigation rails and a disciplined 760px maximum reading column to preserve intimate contemplative cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent atmospheric layers rather than aggressive drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#080A0C` to `#0D0F12` gradient underlays, often showcasing low-contrast night photography of illuminated courtyards.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards):** `#13171C` filled with 80% opacity, bordered by a hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Frosted Bottom Sheets & Elevated Drawers):** `rgba(19, 23, 28, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)` and a top highlight edge of `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` to simulate upward lantern light.
- **Level 3 (Floating Actions & Modals):** Soft ambient glow `0 16px 36px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)` supplemented by a faint emerald or gold halo: `0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces the fluid softness of rounded domes and architectural arches:

- **Pill Geometry (`rounded-full` / Level 3):** Buttons, search inputs, active filter chips, and floating tab indicators follow fully curved pill perimeters to balance the sharp verticality of condensed headings.
- **Card & Sheet Architecture:** Elevated bottom sheets utilize `2rem` top border radii, framing content like prayer alcoves (*Mihrab*).
- **Interactive Micro-Surfaces:** Avatar badges, prayer countdown rings, and iconography containers are circular or rounded-full.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill radius, solid `#D4AF37` fill, deep charcoal text (`#080A0C`), semi-bold. Height of 52px on mobile to guarantee effortless touch response.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Full pill radius, semi-transparent background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), ivory text (`#FBFBF8`).
- **Subtle Tap State:** Downward scale transform to `0.98` with internal gold rim brightening.

### Frosted Bottom Sheets
- Anchored to bottom screen edges with persistent swipe handlebars (`40px x 4px` rounded bar, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`).
- Blurred translucent surface showing dim mosque night vistas underneath.
- Houses daily Hadith reflections, audio players, or surah commentaries.

### Chips & Tags
- Height: 34px, fully pill-shaped.
- Unselected: Dark background (`#13171C`), hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), text `#A4ACB9`.
- Selected: Emerald tone (`#1B4D3E`) with gold border accent (`#D4AF37`) and pure ivory label.

### Input Fields
- Height: 52px, full pill shape.
- Background: `rgba(19, 23, 28, 0.85)` with inset soft shadow.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. On focus, transitions smoothly to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow of `0 0 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- Iconography placed inside margins with minimum 44px tap targets for clear and mic buttons.

### Navigation Indicators
- Minimalist floating bottom bar with pill form, frosted slate backdrop, and micro-dot indicators.
- Active items reveal a muted gold glow behind the icon, while inactive tabs remain muted slate (`#68707D`).

### Knowledge Cards & Verses
- Minimalist dark obsidian panels with subtle linear gradient transitions (`#13171C` to `#0F1216`).
- Integrated verse number badges stamped in gold outline circles.
